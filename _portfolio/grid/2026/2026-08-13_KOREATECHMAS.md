## About the Activity

Dr. Hong-In Won delivered an invited faculty-training lecture for the Future Technology Education Center at Korea University of Technology and Education (KOREATECH). The session, “Building and Applying Multi-Agent Systems in the AI Agent Era,” connected agentic AI concepts with practical research and training applications.

The lecture positioned multi-agent systems as an applied capability for designing, coordinating, and evaluating AI-enabled workflows, with implications for advanced technology education and manufacturing innovation.
