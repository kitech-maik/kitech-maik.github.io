Researcher Profile

Hong-In Won, Ph.D.

Principal Researcher, Korea Institute of Industrial Technology (KITECH)
Head of the Manufacturing AI Cooperation Team

Dr. Hong-In Won is a Principal Researcher at KITECH. Since 2019, he has served as a KITECH research principal investigator, working across manufacturing AI, digital transformation, and data-driven production systems. He received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Design Engineering from Hanyang University and previously worked as a postdoctoral researcher at TU Dresden.

Contact

Research Focus

Manufacturing AI

AI methods and implementation strategies for practical manufacturing innovation and industrial digital transformation.

Digital Twin Systems

Data, simulation, and virtual validation approaches for intelligent production systems.

Data-Driven Production

Process intelligence, quality-oriented analytics, and collaborative deployment of AI in production environments.
